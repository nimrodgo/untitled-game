# Explanation
- When you play the game, you get to choose which character you want to play as
- Each character has their own sets that appear in all markets
- There might be special encounters with cards from another characters
- Each character has a unique starting charm
# Characters
*ordered by complexity*<br>
*just a description of the characters, not their names yet*
## Goldfish
- Starting character, uses gold a lot
- cards that benefit of having a large amount of gold
- use gold to pay for cards
## Manta Ray
- A character focused on "stealth"
- Has cards that interact with the encounter itself. For example:
  - the coin amount
  - the current enemy ability
## Rainbowfish
- starting charm makes already enhanced cards appear in the shop
- a lot of synergy with enhancements and enhanced cards
## Hermit Crab
- Has gems as a resource
- Gems are a resource that stays between turns but gets removed at the end of the encounter
- Some items give gems and some have gem costs
## Diver
- A character that needs air to play cards
- Diver gets 3 air per turn
- Every card not from the diver set costs 1 if it's not instant and 0 if it is
- Diver cards have cost unrelated to whether they are instant or not
